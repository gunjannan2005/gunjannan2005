## 🎯 Profile Summary

I am an **Applied AI Engineer and Distributed Systems Builder** pursuing my B.Tech in Information Technology at **Priyadarshini Engineering College, Nagpur** (RTMNU University, 2023–2027). My technical focus is anchored in **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, **Multi-Modal Document Intelligence**, and **Deterministic Validation Infrastructure** for high-stakes enterprise applications.

Rather than relying on basic prompt interfaces, I treat LLM application development as an applied mathematical and engineering discipline. My research and production systems address three fundamental bottlenecks in modern AI deployment:

1. **Information Retrieval Quality:** Implementing parallel retrieval topologies that synthesize dense semantic vector search (FAISS / MiniLM) and sparse inverted term indices (BM25) using dynamic rank fusion algorithms (RRF).
2. **Deterministic Hallucination Boundaries:** Establishing rigorous confidence-floor gating and source-evidence provenance mechanisms that measurably drive down factual hallucination rates from **8.0% to 2.0%** across standardized benchmark evaluations.
3. **Structured Entity Extraction:** Designing asynchronous, high-throughput parsing pipelines that ingest heterogeneous documents (PDFs, multi-surface scans, institutional spreadsheets) and normalize complex domain entities with sub-second execution times.

I was selected as an **SIH 2025 Grand Finalist (Top 5 Nationally from 500+ institutional contenders)** and recognized as a **State-Level Winner at Patent Gems of Vidarbha (PIC 2025)** by Hon. Union Cabinet Minister Nitin Gadkari.

## 💻 Core Portfolio & Key Systems

### 1. [IntelliRAG — Production Hybrid Retrieval & Document Intelligence Platform](https://github.com/gunjannan2005/Advanced-RAG-learning-system.git)
> **Role:** *Main Solo Architect & Lead Developer*  
> **Repository:** [`gunjannan2005/Advanced-RAG-learning-system`](https://github.com/gunjannan2005/Advanced-RAG-learning-system.git)  
> **Key Benchmark Metrics:** **Recall@5: 0.720 (+2.9% vs. Dense Baseline) · Hallucination Rate: 2.0% vs. 8.0% Sparse Baseline · 95.7% Confidence Calibration**

- **Ingestion Pipeline:** Engineered an asynchronous, multi-format document parser utilizing hierarchy-aware semantic chunking for mixed-format corpora (scanned PDFs, documentation trees, and raw text).
- **Parallel Retrieval Core:** Designed parallel vector and keyword indexing architectures using FAISS (dense semantic vectors via `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`) and BM25 (sparse lexical inverted indices).
- **Multi-Stage Query Engine:** Integrated dynamic intent classification, query expansion, and a conditional cross-encoder reranking layer that operates strictly on ambiguous margins to cut compute overhead by 6×.
- **Production API:** Deployed over **40+ async REST endpoints** with strict Pydantic schemas, comprehensive health metrics, and automated evaluation suites.
- **Tech Stack:** *Python, FastAPI, FAISS, BM25, Scikit-learn, NumPy, SQLite, MiniLM-L6-v2, Docling, Uvicorn*
